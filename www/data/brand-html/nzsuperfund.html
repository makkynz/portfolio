The NZ Super Fund (nzsuperfund.nz)

I am currently employed as a Senior Software Developer at the NZ Super Fund as part of the Data Technology team.

A major piece of work I contributed to was the successful completion of a three-year digital transformation programme, migrating the Fund's investment data platform from a legacy SQL Server environment to a modern architecture built on Snowflake, Azure and Coalesce.

The new platform now underpins the Fund's approximately $95 billion investment portfolio, providing the core data platform for managing positions and transactions.
